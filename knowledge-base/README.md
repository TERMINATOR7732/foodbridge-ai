# FoodBridge AI — Knowledge & Domain Reference

This directory serves as the documentation and domain reference guide for food safety, redistribution standards, and handling protocols implemented in FoodBridge AI.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Advisory & Educational Reference**: This knowledge base contains reference domain documentation and educational guidelines. It is **not** a replacement for official public health food-safety authorities, statutory regulations, or professional physical food inspections. All parameters described herein operate as **demonstration rules** and **reference thresholds** within the software model and **require local regulatory verification** in any production deployment.

## Architectural Connection

The core decision-support rules encoded within FoodBridge AI's local advisory engine (`src/services/mockAnalyzer.ts`, `src/services/matchingService.ts`, and `src/services/assistantService.ts`) mirror established food recovery heuristics:

1. **Food Category Baselines**: Demonstration rules for safe availability windows (e.g. Prepared Meals: same-day redistribution reference threshold of 4–8 hours; Bakery & Bread: 24–72 hours; Fresh Produce: 48 hours).
2. **Temperature & Storage Rules**: Storage verification required before confirming transfers of refrigerated or perishable items (reference threshold: cold holding $\le 5\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$).
3. **Safety Gate Thresholds**: Immediate blocking of stale, expired, or improperly stored surplus food based on deterministic age boundaries.

## Regulatory & Advisory Notice

All food redistribution recommendations generated by FoodBridge AI are advisory decision-support outputs. A designated human coordinator must conduct physical sensory inspection (smell, texture, packaging integrity, and temperature check) prior to food transfer. Real-world food redistribution operations must comply with local food safety legislation, public health codes, and Good Samaritan food donation statutes.
